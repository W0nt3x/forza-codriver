5 · Drive

    
Go to Drive, pick the stage, press Start, then race it in any car at any speed. Every drive is recorded. Press Learn on the stage afterwards and the notes get better.

    
No time to record? The Stages tab lists community stages other players shared. Install one and drive. And if you built a good one, press Share there.

  







  
No voice pack is active, you will hear beeps. Generate one on the Setup or Voice tab (20 seconds), then start the co-driver.
